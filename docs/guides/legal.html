Legal

Walk Me Through It (WMTI) is source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: anyone may read and use the code for non-commercial purposes only. Its Terms say the results are estimates for education, not advice, may not come true and come with no warranty. This page lists where each piece lives.

Note

This is not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review these documents before a wide release.

What is where

  • LICENSE: the official PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 text, unchanged, after the Required Notice line (the copyright year, the copyright holder’s name, Walk Me Through It (WMTI) and the address of the public repository).

  • legal/TERMS.md: the Terms of use, in numbered sections with defined terms (the Terms, the Software, Results, Your Data, the Privacy Notice, the License): eligibility (legal age and capacity, confirmed by agreeing; the agreement box itself only says I have read and agree to the Terms), education only and not advice or a fiduciary relationship, US federal and state (50 states and DC) tax only, hypothetical results with the Monte Carlo statement in capitals, the user’s own risk, the warranty disclaimer and the limitation of liability in capitals (with the carve-out for liability the law does not let us exclude), the data and device section (data loss included), free test software with no support, permitted (noncommercial) use, a release, a covenant not to sue and a light indemnity, governing law with a 30-day informal resolution step and a small-claims carve-out (no arbitration and no class waiver), and the general clauses.

  • legal/PRIVACY.md: the Privacy notice (everything stays on the device; shared devices, exported files, children, your rights, how to clear your data). It has its own Version: line, for readers; only the Terms version asks users to agree again.

  • legal/TRADEMARKS.md: the unregistered marks claimed (the brand name, Walk Me Through It, WMTI) and the third-party names used.

  • legal/NOTICE.md: the third-party components the app ships and their licenses; legal/third-party/: each component’s full license text (numpy, pandas, python-dateutil, pytz, six, Pyodide’s MPL 2.0, CPython’s license, and the Open Font License of each font).

  • SECURITY.md: how to report a security problem, the scope and the safe harbour; copied to the public repository.

  • README.md and pyproject.toml: the copyright sentence, and the package metadata license = "PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0" with license-files = ["LICENSE"].

  • App: frontend/build.py writes LICENSE, legal/*.md and the third-party license texts (one document, Third-party licenses) into legal.<hash>.js; the Terms and agreement step and the menu’s Legal window draw them as plain text (see The app). Every code file and index.html starts with a license header made from the Required Notice line, and the model zip holds LICENSE.

  • Public site: frontend/publish_pages.py copies LICENSE, SECURITY.md, legal/*.md and legal/third-party/*.txt into the public repository and puts the license line, the disclaimer and the security line in its README.md.

The Terms version

The Terms version is the Version: date of legal/TERMS.md, a + and the first six hex characters of the sha256 of the file’s text (line endings made LF first), such as 2026-10-19+1a2b3c, computed by frontend/build.py (termsVersion). Any wording change gives a new version, even when the date stays, so every user is asked to agree again; still change the date when the Terms change, so readers see it. An acceptance records the version, the text’s full sha256, the build id and the time, so it shows which text was accepted.
