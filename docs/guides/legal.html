Legal

Purpose: for anyone who wants to know under what terms WMTI is licensed and used, and where each legal document lives.

Walk Me Through It (WMTI) is source-available under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: anyone may read and use the code for non-commercial purposes only. Its Terms say the results are estimates for education, not advice, may not come true and come with no warranty.

Note

This is not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review these documents before a wide release.

What is where

File

What it holds

LICENSE

The official PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 text, unchanged, after the Required Notice line (copyright year, holder, WMTI and the public repository address).

legal/TERMS.md

The Terms of use: eligibility (legal age, confirmed by agreeing), education only and not advice, US tax only, hypothetical results, your own risk, warranty disclaimer and limitation of liability, data and device, free test software, noncommercial use, a release, a covenant not to sue, a light indemnity, and governing law with a 30-day informal resolution step and a small-claims carve-out (no arbitration, no class waiver).

legal/PRIVACY.md

The Privacy notice: everything stays on the device; shared devices, exported files, children, your rights, how to clear your data. Its Version: line is for readers; only the Terms version asks users to agree again.

legal/TRADEMARKS.md

The unregistered marks claimed (the brand name, Walk Me Through It, WMTI) and the third-party names used.

legal/NOTICE.md and legal/third-party/

The third-party components the app ships, their licenses and each full license text.

SECURITY.md

How to report a security problem, the scope and the safe harbour; copied to the public repository.

README.md and pyproject.toml

The copyright sentence and the package license metadata.

The app bundles these files; the Terms and agreement step and the menu’s Legal window show them as plain text (see The app). Every code file starts with a license header, and the model zip holds LICENSE. The public site copies the same files into the public repository and adds the license line, the disclaimer and the security line to its README.md.

The Terms version

The Terms version is the Version: date of legal/TERMS.md, a + and the first six hex characters of the sha256 of the file’s text (line endings made LF first), such as 2026-10-19+1a2b3c, computed by the frontend build. Any wording change gives a new version, even when the date stays, so every user is asked to agree again; still change the date when the Terms change. An acceptance records the version, the text’s full sha256, the build id and the time.
