Gamer

Purpose: use the garden, levels, quests, awards and mini games in the Gamer style.

Note

For now Gamer is a look only (see Styles and cues): the garden, levels, quests, awards and mini games below are switched off and come back in a later build.

Gamer gives levels and XP for what you do. XP counts answering Walkthrough questions, exploring the results and trying strategies (quests) and finishing mini games. XP never counts how a plan turns out, and nothing here changes a result. Playing never changes the plan.

Garden tab

Garden is a fourth bottom tab that shows only in Gamer style. Pick another style and it goes; your progress stays. In the wide layout, which has no bottom bar, a Garden chip in the Outputs row opens it in the Outputs column.

Part

What it shows

Glass Garden

One bed per Walkthrough topic. A bed opens at its level (About you at 1, then one or two beds a level up to Tax strategies at 7). Its soil is how much of that topic you filled in: a question counts once you answer it or change its input from the example. The plants grow with the latest simulation’s chance of success. The Plan year slider turns the seasons. Every bed and chip is a button that explains itself below.

Tools

Three tools that open with levels; each opens one mini game (see Tools and levels).

Level and XP

Eight levels, from Starter to Legend. A bar shows the XP to the next level, and Next step says what earns more.

Quests

Thirteen ways to explore and try strategies, such as Tour every plot, Try Roth conversions or Compare two plans. Each says why it helps and has Go. The capstone, Retire as soon as you can, asks you to apply an Optimize result and then run Earliest retirement. A High quest shows Unlocks at High at lower efforts. A quest you finished stays done in this scenario.

Awards

Badges for adding plan detail (age, pay, accounts, home, Social Security and more), grouped by area, plus the mini games’ badges. An earned one shows its date; an unearned one says what to add, with Show me.

Greenhouse

The mini games, each with Play and your best stars (out of 3).

Reset progress

Asks first, then clears XP, quests and awards. What is already true does not count again until you do it anew.

Level-up card

Reaching a level opens a card that names the steps that earned it (largest XP first) and what opened. If the Garden is not open, a short note says the garden has news, and the card shows the next time you open the Garden. XP pays once. The garden follows your plan; it never changes it.

Quest map

In Gamer, the Walkthrough’s Your trail reads as a quest map. Each topic is a stage with n of m answered. Each question is a node on the way.

Mini games

Seven short games, three rounds each, with no clock. Play them from the Garden tab’s Greenhouse, or with Quick game on four Walkthrough questions.

  • The games use fixed law figures and example numbers, apart from your benefit table.

  • They change nothing in your plan. The one exception is Claim Age Showdown’s Set my claim age, at Medium and High. It asks first (Change or Cancel), and Undo puts the old age back.

  • Finishing a game earns XP once. Playing again earns nothing more. Close stops a game at any time and costs nothing.

  • Each game ends with “Illustrative, not advice.”

Progress saved in scenario files

  • Progress belongs to the scenario. It is saved with it and goes into its exported file, so importing the file on another device brings it along.

  • Another scenario shows its own progress.

  • Progress that an older version kept on this device moves into the open scenario once.

  • The main app keeps it in the file but does not show it.
