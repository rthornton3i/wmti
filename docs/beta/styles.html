Styles and cues

Purpose: see what each style adds and what the glow cues mean.

Style

What it adds

Accountant

Numbers and tables. This is also the main app’s only style.

Coach

The Next steps card and tips.

Gamer

Everything in Coach, plus levels, quests, awards and mini games.

Change the style in the picker (see The picker).

Cues

Your effort and style choice adds a glow, an edge and a sheen to the panels.

Choice

Cue

Low

A calm blue.

Medium

A warm gold.

High

A hot red.

Accountant

A calm slate.

Coach

A clean green.

Gamer

A pink and violet glow.

  • Cues grow from Low to High and from Accountant to Gamer. Low and Accountant are calm but visible; High and Gamer glow strongly.

  • They change shadows, edges and backgrounds only, so text keeps its AA contrast. They never change a figure, a saved file or a chart color.

  • With reduced motion, they stay still. High contrast, forced colors and print turn them off.
