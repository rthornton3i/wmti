Walk Me Through It (WMTI) user guide

Walk Me Through It (WMTI) is a planner for a household’s money over many years: it projects income, expenses, taxes and account balances year by year, so you can compare the long-term effect of tax strategies, retirement-account strategies and other financial decisions. The app runs the model in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

This is the user guide published with the app (its Documentation menu item): how to use the app, what the model does, and the tax law it applies for the federal return and each state, with the confidence and sources of every figure. Everything here is a model of the law for education only, not financial, tax or legal advice.
