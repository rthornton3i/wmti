The law the model applies for Wyoming, as its law file .config/law/wy.json states it (tax year 2026). How to read the law pages explains the chips and the tables; nothing here is tax advice.

Source

Wyoming levies no personal income tax, estate tax or inheritance tax. Primary sources read on 2026-10-24: Wyoming Legislature LSO, Wyoming Tax Rates and Collections, June 3, 2025 (https://wyoleg.gov/InterimCommittee/2025/03-202506032-01WyomingtaxstructureratesandcollectionsJune32025.pdf: individual income tax 0%, FY24 collections $0); W.S. title 39 (https://wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title39.pdf: chapter 12 holds only W.S. 39-12-101, which bars local income taxes; W.S. 39-19-103 sets the estate tax equal to the federal state death tax credit); 26 U.S.C. 2011(f) (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2012-title26/html/USCODE-2012-title26-subtitleB-chap11-subchapA-partII-sec2011.htm: no credit for decedents dying after 2004); Wyoming Constitution art. 15 sec. 18 (https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Publications/WYConstitution.pdf); Department of Revenue (https://revenue.wyo.gov/: excise, mineral and property taxes only). Second sources: MOAA state report card (https://www.moaa.org/content/state-report-card/wyoming/) and Tax Foundation, State Individual Income Tax Rates and Brackets 2026 (https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/state/state-income-tax-rates-2026/)

Income base and add-backs (state_base)

Confidence: Confirmed primary

The state adds back none of the federal deductions the model tracks (Traditional deposits, Traditional IRA deposits and HSA deposits): it starts from the same income as the federal return.

How the model uses this: The federal deductions the state adds back to the income it taxes (add_back), and how its income base differs from the federal one. More in the model rules.

Sales tax (sales_tax)

Confidence: Confirmed secondary

State sales tax 4%; local sales taxes add 1.39% on average (5.39% combined). A car purchase pays 5.39%; groceries pay 0%. Clothing taxed: yes.

How the model uses this: The sales tax on taxable spending and on a car purchase (vehicle_rate), the rate on groceries and whether clothing is taxed; a move to another state changes the spending by the ratio of one plus each state’s rate, and a car purchase by the difference of the two vehicle rates.
