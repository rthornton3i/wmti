Note: Brackets: 2026 rates under H. 4216: 1.99% on taxable income up to 30,000 and 5.21% above (SCDOR: 5.21% minus 966, the same tax), one schedule for every filing status (RFA fiscal impact statement and SCDOR, primary; news reports agree). Two identical tables so the deduction can differ by filing status. From 2026 South Carolina starts from federal adjusted gross income (H. 4216), so 401(k) deferrals, deductible Traditional IRA deposits and HSA deposits stay deductible.
