Income base and add-backs (state_base)

Confidence: Confirmed primary

The state adds back none of the federal deductions the model tracks (Traditional deposits, Traditional IRA deposits and HSA deposits): it starts from the same income as the federal return.

How the model uses this: The federal deductions the state adds back to the income it taxes (add_back), and how its income base differs from the federal one. More in the model rules.

Income tax brackets (income_brackets)

Confidence: Confirmed primary

Graduated rates from 1.99% to 5.21%. Each rate applies to the taxable income between its threshold and the next; the base is the tax on all the brackets below, so the tax is the base plus the rate times the income over the threshold.

The thresholds are indexed: the law grows them with inflation each year, and so does the model.

Sales tax (sales_tax)

Confidence: Confirmed secondary

State sales tax 6%; local sales taxes add 1.49% on average (7.49% combined). A car purchase pays 1.72%; groceries pay 1.49%. Clothing taxed: yes.

How the model uses this: The sales tax on taxable spending and on a car purchase (vehicle_rate), the rate on groceries and whether clothing is taxed; a move to another state changes the spending by the ratio of one plus each state’s rate, and a car purchase by the difference of the two vehicle rates.
