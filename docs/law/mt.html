Income tax brackets (income_brackets)

Confidence: Confirmed primary

Graduated rates from 4.7% to 5.65%. Each rate applies to the taxable income between its threshold and the next; the base is the tax on all the brackets below, so the tax is the base plus the rate times the income over the threshold.

The thresholds are not indexed: only a new law changes them (legislated).

Note: Montana taxes net long-term capital gains at 3.0% up to 47,500 single / 95,000 joint less ordinary income and 4.1% above, instead of 4.7% and 5.65% (2026 Publication 1 tax tables and MCA 15-30-2103, read 2026-10-24).
