Note: Pension income deduction of up to 49,824 per person for 2026: 36 M.R.S. 5122(2)(M-2) (read 2026-10-24) sets the pension deduction amount at the maximum annual Social Security benefit at full retirement age on 1 January of the tax year, covering employer plan and IRA distributions included in federal AGI; 4,152 x 12 = 49,824 uses the SSA 2026 maximum from secondary sources (ssa.gov refused the fetch), and Maine Revenue Services has not yet published its 2026 figure (2025: 48,216). Approximations: the deduction is reduced by all Social Security and Railroad Retirement benefits received (not modelled, so the exclusion is too large for a person drawing benefits); it phases out over 100,000 of federal AGI above 125,000 single / 250,000 joint from 2025 (M-3, indexed; not modelled); Maine has no age test but distributions that pay the federal 10% early-withdrawal tax do not qualify, so 59.5 stands in. The cap follows the Social Security maximum (which grows each year with the cost-of-living adjustment and wages) ; the model scales it with the indexed brackets (CPI), an approximation.
