Income tax brackets (income_brackets)

Confidence: Confirmed primary (the state’s income tax confidence)

Graduated rates from 4.75% to 9.9%. Each rate applies to the taxable income between its threshold and the next; the base is the tax on all the brackets below, so the tax is the base plus the rate times the income over the threshold.
