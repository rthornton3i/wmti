The app: scenarios and guided flows

Purpose: learn to save and share scenarios, choose how much the app asks, and use the Walkthrough, continued from The app.

Scenarios on Setup

The settings you edit are the open scenario. One panel at the top of Setup holds it and, under it, Scenario files; the other Setup panels follow.

The status line reads:

Status

Meaning

Unsaved changes

The settings differ from the copy saved on this device, or none is saved.

Saved, Saved just now

The settings match the saved copy.

Saved automatically

The Auto-save switch is on.

Tough example

The unedited example household, which runs short of money on purpose. Any edit removes the tag; Load example household brings it back.

Auto-save

  1. Auto-save is on for every new scenario, duplicate and import.

  2. While it is on, each settled edit (after a 0.4 s pause) writes the saved copy, and Save is hidden.

  3. Turn it off and it stays off for that scenario only.

Card buttons

A strip under the status line holds three buttons:

  • Rename changes the name.

  • Duplicate saves a copy as “name_Copy1” (the lowest free number) and opens it. Compare offers the copy as a baseline.

  • Clear empties every setting after two confirmations. Undo brings the settings back.

Scenario files

Button

What it does

Save

Keeps the settings on this device under the open scenario’s name. The limit is 50 scenarios. Hidden while Auto-save is on.

Import

Opens a dialog to choose a file or paste the scenario text, then Open. The file’s settings become the open scenario, named after the file (“Pasted scenario” for pasted text).

Export

Saves name.json as a download.

New

Asks for a name, then Create and start opens an empty scenario in the Walkthrough. Summary and Plots show “Finish setup to see results” until your age is filled in.

A message under the open card always says what happened (“Saved”, “Downloaded”, “Export cancelled”).

An import named like a saved scenario with other settings opens as “name_1” instead of writing over it.

Other scenarios

This list holds every saved scenario except the open one, plus the example household while it is not open.

  1. Tap a row to open it. With unsaved changes and Auto-save off, a dialog asks first: Save, Discard or Cancel.

  2. Use a row’s “…” menu for Duplicate, Rename, Export and Delete. Delete asks once.

  3. The example household has no Rename. Deleting it frees its name until Load example household under Start over opens it again.

  4. The open scenario cannot be deleted.

Deleting a scenario removes it from the list, from Compare and from this device.

Limits and names

  • A scenario holds at most 50 accounts, 50 loans, 20 cars, 20 homes, 20 children, 100 salary-history years, 200 expense rows and 200 rows in any other list.

  • A file with a larger list, or a number too large to hold, is refused with a message naming the field.

  • A name uses letters, digits, spaces, “-”, “_” or “.”. It may not start with “.” or end with “.” or a space.

  • A name may not be “Default profile”, “Current settings” or a Windows device name such as CON, so it works as a file name anywhere.

Undo and reset

  • Undo (the round button at the top bar’s left) steps back through the last 20 settled input states, Clear included. A burst of typing is one step. It starts empty whenever another scenario opens.

  • After a step back, the page goes to the first changed field and tints it for one second.

  • A field that shows a gray default gets a small circular-arrow button once you edit it. It puts the default back, so a blank field follows its source again. Reset counts as an Undo step.

Terms and agreement

A full-screen step shows before the app on a first visit, and again whenever the Terms change. See Legal for the Terms.

  1. Read the four key points and the full Terms, Privacy notice and licenses.

  2. Tick “I have read and agree to the Terms”.

  3. Tap Agree and continue. Decline leaves the app locked.

If browser storage is blocked, the step asks on each load.

Under the Outputs run bar, two lines state that the planner is not advice. A banner (“Example figures still in use for: …”) names settings still holding the example household’s fictional figures.

Effort and style

Effort sets how much the app asks. Style sets how results are shown. Neither changes a result.

Choice

What you get

Low

Pay, savings and monthly spending. See Low effort.

Medium

Adds retirement age, the 401k match, home, cars, loans and Social Security. See Medium effort and Add detail.

High

Every input.

Accountant

Numbers and tables.

Coach

Adds next steps and tips.

Gamer

Adds quests, awards and mini games.

Coach and Gamer add the Next steps card. In the menu’s picker, Risk comfort (Cautious, Balanced, Growth-leaning, or Not set for Balanced) only picks how a Coach check scores; it changes no input or result.

Gamer also gives levels and XP for what you do: answering walkthrough questions, filling in the basics, running the simulation, opening Plots, comparing and searching. XP never counts how a plan turns out. Quests and awards are on the Awards view, and the walkthrough Trail shows each topic as a stage with n of m answered. Progress stays on this device and never goes into a scenario file. Reset progress clears it.

Gamer adds four short mini games, each three rounds with no clock. Play them from Awards, or with Quick game on four walkthrough questions:

  • Bracket Climber (pay): marginal and average tax on a to-scale ladder of the federal brackets.

  • The Bad Decade (savings): the same returns in the opposite order, and what a withdrawal does.

  • Receipt Sort (medical plan): which account generally pays for what: HSA, dependent care FSA, 529 plan or regular money.

  • Claim Age Showdown (claim ages): the break-even age, from your own benefit table.

The games use fixed law figures and example numbers, apart from your benefit table. They change nothing in your plan. The only exception is Claim Age Showdown’s Set my claim age, at Medium and High: it asks first (Change or Cancel), and Undo puts the old age back. Each game ends with “Illustrative, not advice.”

To change them:

  1. Open the menu and tap Effort and style… (Change effort on the Low page).

  2. Drag the energy bar, tap a level, or use the arrow keys. Tap one of the three style buttons.

  3. Tap an (i) for a description. Tap Save to keep the choice, or Cancel to discard it.

At first start the picker follows the Terms with a single Continue. Then the Walkthrough and a tour come.

Each scenario keeps its own effort and style. Opening or importing one with different choices asks which to use: “Use the scenario’s”, “Keep mine” or Cancel. A scenario saved before the pickers opens as High and Accountant.

Below High, Setup notes “Using defaults for N inputs” (hidden inputs that still match the example household) and “Detail added on N pages and inputs” with a Hide it button.

Medium effort and Add detail

At Medium, each Inputs pane, each list sheet and Setup’s Household inputs leave out the High-only inputs. Each ends with an Add detail row. It names how many inputs it would show, the first three, and how many are set.

  1. Tap Add detail to show that pane’s High inputs.

  2. Tap Hide detail to hide them again.

Hidden inputs keep their values and the model still uses them, so results are the same at every effort.

Medium also leaves out the Strategies inputs (a note and an Add detail row replace them), Where surplus cash goes, future home and car purchases or sales, and the visit-based healthcare and Social Security estimate choices.

The menu’s Effort and style… pop-up lists every page and input added this way under Detail added. Each has its own Hide it button.

Low effort

At Low the app asks no home, car, loan or Medicare question. Inputs is one page of cards with no group chips.

Card

What you enter

About you

Your age, whether a partner is part of the plan (then the partner’s age and filing status), your state.

Pay

Yours and your partner’s.

Spending

One monthly number, with a hint on what the plan counts on top of it.

Savings

Savings, Investments, Traditional 401k and Roth 401k.

Children

Yes or No, then how many and each age. A child you expect has a negative age.

Retirement plan, Medical plan

Yes or No. While on, a summary with Change.

More detail

A pointer to Medium effort, with Change effort.

Setup’s Household card is hidden at Low. With two accounts of one kind, that kind is read-only until Medium.

Guided plans

Yes on the retirement or medical plan opens a pop-up with a live summary and its questions (401k share, employer matches, premium). Save keeps the answers as one Undo step; Cancel discards them. Change reopens it. No turns the plan off and keeps its other values.

Spending covers housing, cars and loans

At Low, monthly spending counts rent or mortgage, car and loan payments, property taxes, utilities and food. Health premiums are set with the medical plan. Nothing is counted twice.

When

What happens

A Low user answers or skips the spending question, or taps Skip for now on the welcome

Homes, rent periods, cars and loans that are still the example household’s are removed. Undo my answers puts them back.

A Medium or High scenario with its own homes, cars, loans or utility bills is switched to Low

Nothing changes. The Spending card says “Your plan also counts … on top of this number” and offers Remove them (one Undo step).

A Low user with none of them raises effort

Nothing changes. Setup says the spending counts housing, cars and loans until the next effort change.

You open or import a scenario

Nothing changes.

Walkthrough

“Walk me through it” asks for the main settings one question at a time, over the page.

Start it

  • A new user’s first launch shows the Terms, then the effort and style picker (Low and Coach chosen), then the Walkthrough, then the tour once per device.

  • Later, choose Walk me through it… in the menu (Continue the walkthrough… while paused).

  • A returning visitor sees the picker once (High and Accountant chosen), then the app. Opening or importing a scenario never starts it.

On the example household’s settings it first asks How would you like to start?. Start fresh goes to the welcome. Open my file imports a scenario file and closes the Walkthrough as finished, every question marked Answered.

Raising the effort offers Continue the walkthrough… again and opens on the first question it unlocked. Lowering the effort keeps every answer.

Answer a question

  1. Each control starts with your current setting, tagged “Example value” while it is the example’s.

  2. Change it, then tap Continue (or press Enter, or swipe left). Skip keeps what is shown. Swipe right to go back.

  3. Tap an (i) for the field’s help. Tap Go deeper: … for extra questions on the topic.

  4. Tap Open on a question that lives on an Inputs page. A “Back to the walkthrough” button brings you back.

Answers write into the settings at once. A value out of range (“Age must be between 18 and 100.”) or an emptied number box is refused with a message under the field. Continue on an untouched question writes nothing.

A follow-up question shows only after you say you have the thing, such as home questions after a Yes to owning a home.

Topics

The topics are About you, Work and pay, Savings, Spending, Retirement, Medicare and Health, plus the optional Tax strategies (Roth conversion, backdoor Roth and QCD), offered from the review only. Retirement asks the claim age and the Social Security benefit: Yes takes the table from your statement, and No or not sure estimates it from your pay. It also asks about a move to another state. Go deeper on Spending goes by category on the Expenses page instead. A monthly spending total replaces every monthly expense row with one Everything else row, and one-off rows stay.

Trail and review

  • A thin bar at the top has one segment per topic. Back returns to the previous question.

  • The route button opens Your trail: every topic and question with a status (Answered, Skipped, Now, Not yet). Tap a row to go there.

  • The last screen, Here is your plan, lists each answer with a Change button. It adds “Still from the example household” with an Open button for each item not yet replaced.

  • At Low and Medium it ends with Change effort. At High a row also has Open in Inputs, which pauses the Walkthrough.

  • Finish opens Outputs, Summary.

Leave and resume

The X (or Escape; Escape first closes the trail) asks what to do:

  • Keep going

  • Continue later: the answers stay, and a reload or the menu resumes at the same question.

  • Undo my answers: the settings go back to what they were when the Walkthrough started.

Progress belongs to the open scenario, so switching scenarios switches which Walkthrough resumes. The automatic Monte Carlo waits while the Walkthrough is open.
