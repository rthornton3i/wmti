Walkthrough at each effort

Purpose: see how the Walkthrough and the Inputs change with effort in the beta.

The Walkthrough itself is in The app: scenarios and guided flows. In the beta it also follows your effort.

How long it is

Effort

Welcome says

Questions for the example household

Low

About 3 minutes

13 (9 for a single user with no children)

Medium

About 10 minutes

35

High

As long as you like; stop or skip any time

39

Raising the effort offers Continue the walkthrough… again and opens on the first question it unlocked. Lowering the effort keeps every answer.

First start and review

  • A new user’s first launch shows the Terms, then the effort and style picker (Low and Coach chosen), then the Walkthrough, then the tour once per device.

  • A returning visitor sees the picker once (High and Accountant chosen), then the app.

  • At Low and Medium the review ends with Change effort. At High a row also has Open in Inputs.

Hidden inputs surface

At Low and Medium, some inputs are hidden. They show when something points to them:

  • Open on a Walkthrough question shows the input it asks about.

  • Show me where on a Next steps gap shows the input it names.

Each shown input is listed under Detail added in the picker, with a Hide it button (see Medium effort and Add detail). Hidden inputs keep their values, so results do not change.
