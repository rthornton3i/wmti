Beta: effort and style

Purpose: find what the beta build adds, which is the effort and style choices and Gamer.

The beta app is at https://rthornton3i.github.io/wmti/beta/ . It has everything in the main app, plus the features in this section. The main app at https://rthornton3i.github.io/wmti/ has none of them: it always runs at High effort in the Accountant style.

The beta keeps its own saved data, apart from the main app’s. Scenarios, settings and progress in one do not show in the other. A scenario file from the beta opens in the main app at High effort and keeps its choices.
