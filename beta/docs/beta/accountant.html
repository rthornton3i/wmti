Accountant: source notes and the ledger

Purpose: check where the Summary figures come from, and download them year by year.

Every style shows these on the Outputs tab, Summary. They describe your settings and results; they never change them.

Item

What it shows

Why? on each tile

Why this number?: how the figure is made, the run (runs, seed, dollars, ages, tax year, states), the inputs behind it with Show me, and the law used with its tax year, date and how well it is sourced.

Estimates card

The parts of the plan that rest on unconfirmed figures, each with Why?. It shows only when there are some.

What changed since the last run

The headline figures before and after, and the settings that differ, against the other kept run of the same scenario.

Download ledger (CSV)

The Final report’s year-by-year tables as one CSV file, in whole dollars. Where the file cannot be saved, Copy CSV text gives the text to copy.

At Low only Why? and the Estimates card show. To hide the notes, clear Show source notes and estimates in Effort and style in the menu, then Save. The ledger button stays.

The CSV starts with a byte-order mark so Excel opens it as it is. Read it in Python with encoding="utf-8-sig".
