Coach: Next steps and risk comfort

Purpose: use the Next steps card, which Coach and Gamer show on the Outputs tab.

Next steps

A Next steps card follows the tiles. It lists up to three gaps found in your inputs. It shows before any Monte Carlo.

  • An unpaid employer match.

  • A child without a 529.

  • No HSA, Roth conversion or backdoor Roth.

At Low only the match gap shows.

Each gap shows what is missing, then, after a check, its result in a Checked: box, then one row of buttons, then What was tried.

Button

What it does

Show me where

Opens the input. At Low it opens the retirement plan setup. At Medium it shows an input that was hidden.

Check

Tests a few values on the same random draws, with progress and Cancel.

Apply

Shows after a check. Writes the best value as one Undo step. The result goes dim, with Apply off, once the settings change.

Not now

Hides a gap. Show N hidden brings it back.

Nothing changes until you tap Apply. While the card shows a person’s match gap, Insights leaves out that person’s unused-match line.

Risk comfort

Risk comfort is in the picker: Cautious, Balanced, Growth-leaning, or Not set (Balanced). It only picks how a check scores better. It changes no input or result.
