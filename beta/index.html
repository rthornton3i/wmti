    Tough example

    This example household runs out of money in many of the Monte Carlo runs, so its first results look like a plan in trouble. That is on purpose: it shows what a struggling plan looks like. Its figures are fictional. Change them, or start a new scenario, to try your own.

    About

    Walk Me Through It (WMTI), a retirement income planner. Everything runs on this device. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; your settings stay in this browser.

    This planner gives estimates for education only; it is not financial, tax or legal advice. It models United States federal tax and the income taxes of the 50 states and the District of Columbia for the tax year shown below (Law data) only. The example household's figures are fictional.
