# Security policy

This policy covers Walk Me Through It (WMTI), offered by [BRAND NAME].

## Reporting

Email [SECURITY EMAIL] or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository. Please include the build
id (Menu, About), your browser and the steps that show the problem. We aim to acknowledge a report within 7 days; no
bounty is offered.

## Scope

- The published site and the installed app at the GitHub Pages address of [PUBLIC REPOSITORY URL].
- The shareable zip of the app.
- The desktop dashboard.

## Out of scope

- Issues that need physical access to a device or a browser profile.
- Self-XSS by pasting your own scenario.
- Vulnerabilities in Pyodide, numpy, pandas, Streamlit or browsers: report them upstream, and tell us too.
- The private preview on claude.ai: report it to Anthropic.

## Supported versions

Only the latest published build. Reload when the app offers "New version ready".

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research that avoids privacy violations and service disruption will not be pursued.

## Disclosure

We fix the problem, publish the fix and note it in the public repository's README.
