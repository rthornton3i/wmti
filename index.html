    About

    Walk Me Through It (WMTI), a retirement income planner. Everything runs on this device. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere; your settings stay in this browser.

    This planner gives estimates for education only; it is not financial, tax or legal advice. It models United States federal tax and the New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania and Florida income taxes for the tax year shown below (Law data) only. The example household's figures are fictional.
